# Paul Edward Harris 4th

Independent AI/ML learner and researcher (also written Paul E. Harris IV). I learn machine learning by building real projects with AI as my development partner — then working through the errors until I understand what I built.

**How I work:** idea → research → prompting → generated code → running it → hitting errors → investigating → correcting → testing → documenting. My projects are built with heavy AI assistance; I direct, run, debug, and verify the work, and I'm learning each part from the inside out.

### How I think

- **Ownership.** I didn't come up through the usual route, and I don't let that decide what I can build.
- **Chaos, not clockwork.** Small inputs shift big outcomes, like the Lorenz system. That's what my research studies.
- **Build in public.** End to end, tested, real results shared, including what didn't work.
- **Verify before believing.** Claims from media, institutions, or my own models get checked, not accepted.

### Projects

- 🌀 [SynechismCore](https://github.com/seek-k-nowledge/SynechismCore) — Technical report: [*Continuous-Time Latent Dynamics for Long-Horizon Chaotic Forecasting under Irregular Sampling*](https://github.com/seek-k-nowledge/SynechismCore/blob/master/paper/Harris_2026_Continuous-Time_Latent_Dynamics_Irregular_Sampling.pdf) (Oct 2026). On Lorenz-63 under four sampling regimes, SynechismV23, a continuous-time latent neural ODE architecture I designed, had the longest valid prediction time in all four against Transformer, LSTM and Mamba-style baselines, with a modest lead over the LSTM. Next: stronger baselines, then arXiv and a 2027 workshop submission.
- 🧠 [GraphRAG Agent](https://github.com/seek-k-nowledge/enterprise-graphrag-agent) — Working prototype (v0.x), actively developed toward production. Self-grounding knowledge graph pipeline with cited answers. Roadmap: authentication, security hardening, claim typing, load testing.
- 🛰️ [Firm Radar](https://github.com/seek-k-nowledge/firm-radar) — Working prototype (v0.1). A private narrative-intelligence operations room for each client: threat radar, a crowd check that flags coordinated inauthentic behavior, network and spread maps, and weekly briefs with confidence levels.
- 📡 [Narrative Radar](https://github.com/seek-k-nowledge/narrative-radar) — Working prototype (v0.1). A real-time news monitor that flags when a topic suddenly surges, across news, social, viral trends and video. Built to research my podcast, Listening Between the Lies.
